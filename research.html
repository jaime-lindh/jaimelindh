02

Publications

Chile, 1990–2014

Polarization and Electoral Incentives: The End of the Chilean Consensus Democracy, 1990–2014

with Jorge Fábrega & Jorge González · Latin American Politics and Society, 60(4), 49–68, 2018

Relying on spatial voting theory to uncover latent ideological dimensions from survey data between 1990 and 2014, this study finds patterns of gradual polarization starting at least ten years before the collapse of consensus — driven by demobilization of the political center that misaligned politicians from their platforms, particularly in the center-left. Helps explain the process behind the 2015 electoral system reform.

Chile, 1990–2017

La fragilidad de los consensos. Polarización ideológica en el Chile post Pinochet

with Jorge Fábrega & Jorge González · Revista de Ciencia Política (Santiago), 39(1), 99–127, 2019

Using CEP public opinion survey data and Poole's (1998) methodology to estimate ideology indirectly, this article documents political, socioeconomic, and generational polarization in Chile between 1990 and 2017 — including a socioeconomic ideological gap widening after 2005, and a generational split emerging after 2011.
